## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

The physics of the batch holds: the viscosity law is tested against an independent IAPWS R12-08 implementation,
the Traugott trend against hand values, the harmonic-mean face against a two-cell hand solve, and every one of
nine mutations that touches a formula or a cited number is caught by a test (section 4). Heat-off presets other
than the Gulf keep their `stateHash` to the bit; rewind with heat on is bit-identical; mass is conserved per
material on every preset at 1e-14. The screen check passes at the five sizes with 0 console errors and 0 external
requests. Two things are not honest enough to go to John's check as they stand, and both are wording:
(B-1) the Hart mud makes the Gulf preset's shallow section overpressured by 1 to 2 ppg (9.4 to 10.5 ppg at
500 ft below the sea floor, where it was 8.6 ppg hydrostatic), exactly in the depth range where Hart's own well is

problem "below 4,900 ft" when the model now disagrees with its source above it; (B-2) the labels say "only partly
calibrated", which claims a calibration that never happened. The minimum fix is text in three places. Everything
else is non-blocking.

## 5. Findings

### BLOCKING (text only; no physics or code change is asked for)

- **B-1. The Hart mud over-pressures the shallow Gulf section, where the source is hydrostatic, and nothing says so.**
  `scripts/review/aud-06/shallow.mjs`, Windows node 26, Gulf end of run, pore pressure (ppg EMW) and u / L at 500 ft
  below the sea floor, Hart mud vs the North Sea mud put back by override (which reproduces main's hash):
  col 100: 9.65 (u/L 0.17) vs 8.59 (0.01); col 150: 10.45 (0.36) vs 8.62 (0.02); col 200: 10.49 (0.42) vs 8.61 (0.02);
  col 250: 9.35 (0.65) vs 8.61 (0.07). At 2,000 ft: 10.2 to 12.2 ppg vs 8.7 to 8.8. Hart, Flemings and Deshpande fitted
  phi0 = 0.40 and beta on the hydrostatic part of Eugene Island 330 (above about 1,500 m), so the model now
  contradicts its source in the very interval the coefficients come from. The cause is not the paper: the mud
  permeability law `log10 k = kA + kB phi` (tuned, `params.ts:103-106`) was tuned with phi0 0.63; at phi 0.40 it gives
  a surface mud 70 times less permeable (10^-18.8 against 10^-17 m^2), so the top of the column no longer drains.
  This is the same mechanism behind the rising cap count (15.6k -> 36k), the extra slope failures with failure on
  (N-3) and the thinner welds. It is reported nowhere: `physics-references.md:386-392` (AUD-3 section), the Gulf
  preset note (`presets.ts:79`), `GULF_NOT_CALIBRATED` (`audit.ts:82-83`), the checks-panel line
  (`checks/index.tsx:32`) and D26.4 all describe the extrapolation "below 4,900 ft" as the limit and say nothing
  about the shallow section. Minimum fix: one sentence in each of `physics-references.md` AUD-3 "Limits",
  `decisions.md` D26.4 outcome and the Gulf preset note, with the numbers above (and the Linux ones if they differ),
  stating that the shallow section is 1 to 2 ppg over hydrostatic because the tuned permeability law was not
  re-fitted, that Hart's well is hydrostatic there, and that nothing was retuned; and a Backlog line (roadmap) for
  re-fitting `kA` / `kB` to a published Gulf permeability-porosity relation (Yang & Aplin 2010 is already named as
  the candidate) with its own review. Do not retune now (house rule).
- **B-2. "Only partly calibrated to the Gulf" claims a calibration.** `audit.ts:82` (`GULF_NOT_CALIBRATED`, shown on
  the pseudo-well, the planned well, the PNG and the CSV) and `checks/index.tsx:32`. Taking two coefficients from a
  paper is a citation, not a calibration: no Gulf section, overburden or pressure was fitted, and the Traugott check
  still fails (gap 1.2 ppg at the default pseudo-well in Chrome). D26.4 asked for "label the Gulf preset's densities
  as uncalibrated". Minimum fix: "Not calibrated to the Gulf: the mud compaction coefficients are Hart et al. (1995)
  Gulf shale values (one well, fitted above 4,900 ft), the sand is the North Sea curve and the mud permeability is
  tuned, so the overburden and the fracture gradient read low (Traugott check) and the shallow section reads over
  hydrostatic." Update `tests/auditLabels.test.ts` and `tests/docs.test.ts` strings if they quote it.

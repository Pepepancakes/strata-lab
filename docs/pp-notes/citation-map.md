# Citation map (LIT-PP-3)

Every page citation in `docs/pp-literature.md`, by section and by source, with the deep-read note that backs it. Generated from the text by a throwaway script (kept outside the repo) that reads every `KEY p. N`, `KEY pp. N-M` and `KEY pdf p. N` in the document; regenerate it the same way if the review changes. Figure, equation and table numbers ride on the page citation they follow in the text and are not listed separately.

Totals: 365 citation instances in the text, 99 distinct (source, page or page range) pairs, 75 distinct pages across the ten sources. Sources named in the papers but not read are marked "(to verify; not read)" in the review and are not counted here. Regenerated after LIT-PP-3b (Hart p. 45 to p. 46 for Eqs. 1-3, Fig. 2 and the coefficients; Alberty & McLean p. 434 to p. 435): the three totals are unchanged.

- HCS: Eq. 3 (Gassmann) was not rendered; the review cites Eqs. 1-2 and 4 and the text (pp. 4-10, 14-15). Preprint, not peer reviewed.

The Gulf preset and D27.1 cite them to p. 46; LIT-PP-4 confirmed p. 46 on the page image (footer "46 GEOLOGY, January 1995"), and the deep read's p. 45 was corrected in LIT-PP-3b.

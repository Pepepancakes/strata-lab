# Pore pressure reading notes

One note per paper, each with a claim-and-quote list in `refs/` (`{"id", "claim", "page", "quote"}`, quotes of at most two sentences). Written from full texts for the pore-pressure literature review (LIT-PP, D27) so the synthesis card can check M4's overburden, pore pressure and fracture gradient against recommended practice, and answer what happens near and below salt. Format follows `docs/salt-notes/`.

LIT-PP-2b (practice and salt papers):

- `iogp608.md`: IOGP Report 608 (2022), Recommended practice for pore pressure and fracture gradient analysis (refs/iogp608.json). Section 4 is the checklist of what a pre-drill PPFG forecast must show.
- `hauser-salt-sealed.md`: Hauser 2020, Pore pressure estimation in salt-sealed formations, Marine and Petroleum Geology 111, 105-115 (refs/hauser-salt-sealed.json)
- `hauser-spe2020-stress.md`: Hauser 2020, Evaluating an integrated physical model for borehole-leakoff pressures, SPE Journal, SPE 204453 (refs/hauser-spe2020-stress.json)
- `hauser-couzens-effective-stress.md`: Hauser and Couzens-Schultz, Can compaction modeling and pore pressure prediction be simplified by the definition of effective stress? SSRN preprint 5128263, not peer reviewed (refs/hauser-couzens-effective-stress.json)

LIT-PP-2a (fracture-gradient papers) adds its notes here on its own branch; the synthesis card reconciles this file if both create it.
